## Steps
1. `get_gear_plan` (levels ahead: 2 by default; more when the player plans a dungeon run a few levels up). Each slot has what is worn, upgrades scored for the spec with their gain, when each can be used and got, every source, and the best enchant.
2. For the top picks, `show_cards` with their references (icon, stats, gain, where to get them), `get_details` when the player wants every source or prices, and `show_map` on the zone of a quest giver, vendor or dungeon entrance.

## A good answer
- The few upgrades with the biggest gain the player can get soonest, by slot, with where to get them (quest, vendor, boss and drop chance, crafting) and from which level.
- Free or cheap ones first (quest rewards, crafted with the player's own professions), then dungeon drops with their chances.
- Enchants worth buying at this level, if any.
- Said once: Blizzard shows only what is worn, so an upgrade may already be in the bags; the weights are ForeverDB's for the spec (assumed when the character has no talent points yet).

The player's own words come first: when they ask for something different from these steps, do what they ask.
