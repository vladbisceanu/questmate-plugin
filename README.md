# Forever Questmate

A friend for your World of Warcraft: Forever adventure. Ask where to go next, what a quest wants, which talents to take, what gear to chase, which dungeons suit your level, or how your last play session went, and Questmate answers from your character: its level, gear, quest log, talents, professions and reputation, read from Blizzard's World of Warcraft API, with game data from ForeverDB.net.

Look up any character by name without signing in. Sign in with Battle.net to use your own characters without naming them, keep a history of your play sessions, and save how you like to be coached: your focus, a guide you follow, the talent build you want. No Forever character yet? Choose "Use the demo account" when you connect to try everything on a real character.

It reads; it never changes anything in your game or account. Not made or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

## Install

In Claude Code:

```
/plugin install forever-questmate --marketplace vladbisceanu/questmate-plugin
```

Then connect it from the Connectors tab and sign in with Battle.net, or choose the demo account.

## What's inside

- The connection to Questmate at `https://questmate.bisceanu.games/mcp`, with Battle.net sign-in.
- Skills that walk through common goals: planning a play session, completing a zone, preparing for a dungeon, choosing gear, levelling a profession, taming a hunter pet, and following a guide.
